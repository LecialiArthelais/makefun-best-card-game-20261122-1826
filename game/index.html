You are the Shorebound. Rill, Tidecaller of the Deep, is waiting. Mouse or keyboard — no account, no network, just the sea.

How to Play

  1. Each hand, play 4 cards. Click a card, then click a lane — or keys 1–5, ← →, Enter.
  2. Cards cost Surge. You start with 2, gain +1 a turn, or bank a card for +2. Rill’s Surge is always visible.
  3. When the tide turns, lanes score. Higher strength takes the lane’s favour (Shallows 1, Mid 2, Deep 3). Win a lane by 4+ and the loser’s hull takes the excess as damage.
  4. Combos: Sails in neighbouring lanes give each other +1 (fleet). Cards downstream of a friendly card gain +1 (riding the current). Floodtide gives a whole lane +2; Undertow takes 2 from the rival lane. Hooks drag a rival card toward the Shallows.
  5. The Tidecaller (cost 3, strength 6) flips the current — once per game, and both sides can see it coming.
  6. Win at 15 favour, or by sinking Rill’s hull to 0.
